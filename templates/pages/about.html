Democratizing career opportunities for every Indian student

NamoLead was created to ensure that no student in India misses a career-defining internship, scholarship, or virtual job simulation simply because they didn't know it existed.

01 — Definition

What is NamoLead?

NamoLead is a verified, student-first career opportunity discovery platform designed specifically for college students and recent graduates across India.

Instead of drowning in clickbait telegram channels, bloated WhatsApp forwards, and paywalled job boards, students use NamoLead to access a curated, daily-updated catalog of Fortune 500 virtual simulations, corporate internships, government defense openings, hackathons, and national scholarships.

Our strict promise: Every single listing links directly to the verified official portal. Zero registration fees, zero gatekeeping, and zero spam.

02 — The Origin

Aim to Start NamoLead

While premier metro colleges often have established training and placement pipelines, thousands of talented engineers, arts, and commerce students in tier-2, tier-3 cities and regional institutions face severe information asymmetry.

Prestigious virtual simulations (by JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, BCG), government defense fellowships, and high-impact student grants are 100% free and open to everyone — yet most students discover them weeks after the deadline has passed.

NamoLead was started with one urgent aim: to level the playing field. To provide an indexed, fast, and transparent platform where Indian students can Learn, Lead, and Grow without barriers.
